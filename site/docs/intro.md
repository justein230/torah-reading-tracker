The mobile and desktop apps store your readings on your device and open without a login. To run the tracker in a browser on your own server, follow [Docker setup](deploy/docker-password.md).

## Log a reading

1. Open **Reading Log**, then expand **Add a Reading**.
2. Choose a reading type, select the portion and aliyah or passage, and enter the date.
3. Select **Add Reading** to save it. Open **Overview** to see your progress.

You can [back up your readings](data/backup.md) or [restore them from a backup](data/restore.md) in **Settings**.
